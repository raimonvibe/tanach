Important: Tanach Reader is a fully static website. We do not collect personal data from users and do not require registration or login credentials.

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how Tanach Reader ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit our website. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data we collect

2.1 No personal data

Tanach Reader does not collect personal data from users. We do not require:

  • Registration or an account
  • Login credentials
  • Email addresses
  • Names or other identifying information

2.2 Automatically collected data

As with most websites, the following data may be collected automatically by your browser or hosting provider:

  • IP address: For technical purposes (server logs)
  • Browser type and version: For compatibility purposes
  • Pages visited: For technical analysis
  • Date and time of visit: In server logs

This data is not linked to your identity and is used only for technical purposes.

3. Use of data

Automatically collected data is used solely for:

  • Operating the website
  • Technical support and troubleshooting
  • Improving the user experience
  • Security purposes

4. Cookies and local storage

4.1 Cookies

Tanach Reader does not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies. We do not use cookies for marketing or advertising purposes.

4.2 Local storage (LocalStorage)

The website may use browser localStorage for:

  • Caching text data for faster load times
  • Storing user preferences (where applicable)

This data is stored only locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

5. Third-party services

5.1 Content delivery networks (CDN)

We use the following third-party services:

  • Font Awesome CDN: For icons. This service may use cookies or tracking technology. See their privacy policy for more information.
  • Cloudflare CDN (via cdnjs): For delivering Font Awesome files.

5.2 Data sources

The website fetches text data from:

  • Sefaria.org: For authentic Jewish texts. We use their public API. See their terms of use for more information.
  • HebCal.org: For Jewish calendar data. We use their open-source library (@hebcal/core).

These services may have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

6. Hosting and servers

Tanach Reader is hosted on:

  • Vercel: For static website hosting. Vercel may maintain standard server logs. See Vercel's privacy policy for more information.

7. Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Because we do not collect personal data, the risk of data breaches is minimal.

8. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Receive information about what data we collect
  • Access your data (where applicable)
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of data
  • Object to processing of data

Because we do not collect personal data, most of these rights do not apply. If you have questions, please contact us.

9. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We recommend checking this page periodically.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us at:

  • Email: info@raimonvibe.com
  • Website: https://tanach.vercel.app

11. Governing law

This privacy policy is governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands and the European Union, in particular the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Summary: Tanach Reader is a privacy-friendly website that does not collect personal data. You can use the site without concerns about your privacy.
